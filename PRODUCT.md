# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to Stephen's personal site (wustep.me) — friends, colleagues, and internet passersby curious about what he reads. They arrive casually, browse covers, maybe flip open a book for notes and quotes, on phone or desktop.

## Product Purpose

A personal, interactive bookshelf sharing Stephen's favorite books with notes, quotes, and links. Success: a visitor lingers, opens a book or two, and leaves with a recommendation — and a sense of Stephen's taste.

## Brand Personality

Warm, literary, unhurried — a quiet library. Editorial voice (lowercase masthead, italic serif asides), tactile interactions (books lift off the shelf and open). Personality shows through craft and restraint, not decoration. The register sits between brand and product: expressive at first glance, product-grade in use.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated landing pages ("drop AI-isms" is an explicit project goal)
- SaaS chrome: hero metrics, gradient text, icon-heading-text card grids
- Goodreads-style database clutter — this is a curated shelf, not a catalog UI
- Twee skeuomorphism: wood textures and page-curl gimmicks beyond the one beloved book-open animation

## Design Principles

1. **Covers carry the color.** Chrome stays quiet and warm; the book covers are the palette.
2. **The book is the hero interaction.** The lift-and-open animation is sacred; everything else defers to it.
3. **Editorial, not templated.** Type and spacing do the talking; no scaffolding (eyebrows, badges, gradients) without a reason.
4. **Product-grade under the brand skin.** Fast, keyboard-accessible, no layout shift, touch-first.
5. **Both rooms matter.** Light (day library) and dark (evening library) themes get equal craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere (Framer Motion and CSS animations).
- Visible focus states; full keyboard navigability for cards and modal.
- Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast in both themes; 44px touch targets on mobile.
